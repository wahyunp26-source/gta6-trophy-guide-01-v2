About & sources

How this guide was made

Every guide here is written in our own words for this entry, from the official trophy names, descriptions and grades as PlayStation publishes them (via Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on all 55). Each guide cites its sources beside it, with the date each source was read, so a reader sees where each fact comes from. A specific location or step with no source earns no trust — and this guide does not invent one.

Trophies whose trigger no guide has pinned down yet are marked not confirmed yet — best lead, not a sure method, instead of presenting a guess as fact. The game launched 19 November 2026, so early guides are still being corrected; this list was checked on 19 November 2026.
